Who we are

Hyphae Mail is operated by hyphae ("we"). It is a web service that drafts emails with AI and sends them from your own Gmail account, after you approve them or automatically on a schedule you set. Contact: %VITE_SUPPORT_EMAIL%.

The short version

  • Hyphae Mail asks Google for one permission: send email as you. It cannot read, list, search or delete your mail.
  • To send while you are away, our server keeps Google's refresh token for your account, encrypted. You can delete it, and revoke access, with one button.
  • The AI sees only what you give it: your instructions and the notes you write about each recipient. It never sees anything from your Gmail.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use Google data for anything except sending the messages you ask us to send. We do not use it to train AI models.

Google user data we access

Google permissionWhat hyphae uses it for
Your account's email address (openid, email) To show which Gmail account is connected and to set the From address on messages you send.
Send email on your behalf (gmail.send) To deliver the emails you approved, or that your automations are set to send, from your own address to the recipients you chose. This is the only Gmail operation Hyphae Mail performs.

Hyphae Mail does not request access to read, list, search, modify or delete messages, labels, drafts, contacts, calendar, Drive files or any other Google data.

How your Google access is handled

When you click "Continue with Google", Google gives our server a refresh token. We encrypt it (AES-256-GCM) before it is stored; the encryption key is held in the server's environment, not in the database. The server exchanges it for short-lived access tokens, which it holds in memory, and uses them only to call Google: the Gmail API to send an email you approved or an automation you created. Tokens are never sent to the AI provider or any other party. When you press "Disconnect and delete my data", we revoke the token with Google and delete it.

The landing, privacy and terms pages load no third-party resources.

Sharing, sale and advertising

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or give it to data brokers. No person at hyphae reads your messages or your Google data, and we do not use it to train any AI or machine-learning model. The only parties that receive data are Google and the AI provider, as described above, to carry out the sends you request, and anyone we are legally required to disclose information to.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hyphae Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing sending feature you asked for, we do not transfer it to others except as necessary to provide that feature or to comply with law, and we do not allow humans to read it.

Retention and deletion

  • Google access: revoke at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or with the Disconnect button.
  • Disconnect: revokes the token and deletes your account record, automations, drafts and suppression list immediately. The action log keeps a line recording the disconnect.
  • Sent email: messages already delivered sit in Gmail (yours and your recipients') under those users' control.

Security

Refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies. Every change to your account needs a request header a third-party site cannot set. No system is perfectly secure; if you suspect a problem, disconnect and contact us.

Children

hyphae is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If a change affects how we use Google user data, we will seek your consent again where Google's policies require it.
