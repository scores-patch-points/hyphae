Privacy Terms Open Hyphae Mail

Email that writes itself, sent from your own Gmail.

Tell Hyphae Mail what an email should say and who it is for. An AI model drafts it, you approve it (or let an automation send it for you), and it goes out from your own Gmail address as a normal email.

Open Hyphae Mail

You sign in with Google. Hyphae Mail asks for one permission: to send email as you.

How it works

  1. You describe the email. Write instructions, such as “thank them for yesterday’s call and propose Thursday”, and list the recipients, with a note on each if you like.
  2. AI drafts one email per recipient. The model sees only your instructions and your notes. It never sees your mailbox, because Hyphae Mail cannot read it.
  3. You stay in control. By default every draft waits in a queue for you to edit and approve. Automations can send on their own, with a daily cap, a suppression list and a log of everything sent.

What Hyphae Mail asks Google for

Send email on your behalf (gmail.send), and your account's email address so the app can show which account is connected.

Hyphae Mail cannot read, list, search or delete any message in your mailbox, and it does not touch your contacts, calendar or files. To send while you are away, our server keeps Google’s refresh token, encrypted; one button revokes it and deletes your data. Details are in the privacy policy.

Who it is for

People who send many personal, one-to-one emails that follow a pattern: follow-ups, introductions, check-ins, reminders. Recipients should be people who expect to hear from you. It is not for bulk marketing or cold outreach at scale; sends are capped at 100 a day.
